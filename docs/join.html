Join Pack 1125

Open to all youth in kindergarten through 5th grade — boys and girls welcome.

Three easy steps

How to Join

Contact Us

Email admin@pack1125.org and tell us about your Scout — name, grade, and school. We'll answer any questions.

Visit a Meeting

Come to a Thursday meeting — pack meeting on the 1st Thursday or den meeting on the 3rd Thursday, 6:30–7:30 PM at Covington-Harper Elementary School, Dumfries VA. See what we're about firsthand.

Complete the Application

Create an account at my.scouting.org, fill out the youth application, and we'll walk you through registration, dues, and getting your Scout's handbook and uniform.

Prefer to start online? Scouting America's BeAScout site lists packs near you (search ZIP 22026) and takes applications online.

Dues & Fees

Pack 1125 dues for this year are to be announced — email admin@pack1125.org for current rates. Dues cover awards, advancement materials, program supplies, and pack activities. On top of that, Scouting America registration is $85 and the NCAC participation fee is $80 for first-year Cub Scouts (adult registration is a reduced $65).

Need help with costs? The NCAC First-Year Full Financial Support Program can cover fees for families with limited means — learn more or apply here.

Uniform

Scouts wear the Cub Scout uniform with pride: Class A (field uniform) or Class B (pack tee) as designated for each activity. Each Scout also needs the handbook for their rank, available through the Scout Shop.

Family Involvement

Cub Scouting is powered by volunteers. Parents and guardians support activities, serve in leadership roles, and reinforce Scouting values at home — and share unforgettable experiences with their Scouts.

We need adult volunteers to help fill leadership positions so we can keep providing a reliable, rewarding Scouting experience. Ask us how you can help.

Per Scouting America requirements, adults must remain on the premises during pack meetings.
