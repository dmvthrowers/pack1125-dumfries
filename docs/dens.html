Our Dens

One den per grade, kindergarten through 5th. Dens meet on the 3rd Thursday of each month.

First things first: every new Scout starts with the Bobcat achievement — the Scout Oath, Law, motto, sign, salute, and handshake — before diving into their rank adventures.

Dens by Rank

Lion — Kindergarten

First steps into Scouting: short, fun adventures that introduce young Scouts to teamwork and the outdoors — with a parent or guardian right alongside.

Tiger — 1st Grade

Curiosity-driven adventures where Tigers explore their community and try new skills, always with their adult partner.

Wolf — 2nd Grade

Wolves take on bigger challenges — fitness, safety, and service — building confidence one adventure at a time.

Bear — 3rd Grade

Bears dig deeper: camping skills, crafts, and citizenship as they grow into more independent young leaders.

🏕

Webelos — 4th Grade

"We'll Be Loyal Scouts" — Webelos start exploring what comes next, with activity badges that preview the Scouts BSA adventure ahead.

Arrow of Light — 5th Grade

The capstone Cub Scout rank: Arrow of Light Scouts lead, camp, and prepare to cross over into a Scouts BSA troop.

All dens come together for the pack meeting on the 1st Thursday of each month, 6:30–7:30 PM.
