Pack Calendar

Meetings, events, and pack activities — always up to date. The pack's schedule may change during the year, so check here before heading out.

Calendar not loading? The regular meeting schedule is below, or email admin@pack1125.org for upcoming dates.

Regular Meeting Schedule

Pack Meeting

1st Thursday of each month, 6:30–7:30 PM. All dens together at Covington-Harper Elementary School, Dumfries VA.

Den Meeting

3rd Thursday of each month, 6:30–7:30 PM. Dens meet by rank at Covington-Harper Elementary School, Dumfries VA.

Season

Regular meetings run October through January. Special events like the Pinewood Derby and fundraisers are added as dates are set.

A note on venue: our meeting location may change as the pack grows. Always check the calendar above for the latest location before heading out.
